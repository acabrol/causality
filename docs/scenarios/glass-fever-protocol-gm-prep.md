# Glass Fever Protocol - Game Master Prep

This original **Causality** scenario is recommended for beginners. It teaches the game twice: first as a starter investigation without active `Time Offender` pressure, then as a complete demonstration with Ilya Voss and his `Counter-System`.

## Table Validation

- Status: scenario played and confirmed playable.
- Tested configuration: 2 players, starter mode without a `Time Offender`.
- Observed duration: about 4 hours.
- Feedback: the players discovered the story without the GM playing an enemy taking adversarial actions during the session.

## Scenario Premise

In the `Now` of 2142, sealed cities still survive the consequences of Glass Fever, a lung-crystallization disease that appeared in Toulouse in 2117. The `Investigators` receive a simple mission: understand whether the epidemic was inevitable, identify its true source, and return with enough `Evidence` to stabilize a `Now` where a cure exists without erasing their reality.

The visible false culprit is **Les congénères de la liberation** (`L.C.L.B`), a small public-ritual and animal-liberation group formed around four Toulouse activists. The true culprit is Doctor Ilya Voss, a former biological virology expert who became the `Investigators'` physician because of the crisis and uses a `Counter-System` to protect his own origin.

The scenario is limited to Toulouse. Like causal depth, the `System's` spatial radius depends on available energy for reopening causal probabilities in spacetime. The `Investigators` may consult consequences from elsewhere, but only causes within Toulouse can be reopened, changed, and merged.

### The System and the Causality Gate in This Protocol

The [Causality setting foundation](../universe-and-system.md) states that time does not exist: Investigators perceive causal chains that the System can unwind and replay. This device combines a quantum computer with the mass and energy properties of a micro black hole, a fictional scientific application of the **general causality theorem**.

For this mission, the System opens a **Causality Gate** centered on the sealed cities' **Main Timeline**. The **Time Flow** is the accessible structure; **Branches**, also called **Branched Timelines**, allow examination and modification of the Toulouse causes of Glass Fever. Rings are control interfaces. **Rewind Dice** represent dark-energy capsules committed to attempting to open these Branches.

The Investigators must then perform a **Merge** onto the Main Timeline to rejoin the **Now** of 2142. An intervention may destroy cure evidence or the causes making the team itself possible. Experienced but unintegrated Branches increase cognitive load, measured by **Mental Load**, risking madness. The Toulouse radius, Rewind Dice costs, and scenario rules still apply.

## Core Game Master Truth

- `L.C.L.B` did not release the disease.
- `L.C.L.B` becomes visible at [TU18](#tu18), raiding a downtown Toulouse pet shop and releasing the caged animals.
- Its only actual connection to the virology R&D laboratory is vandalism at [TU17](#tu17), after Voss is hired: the group releases experimental animals and wrecks equipment.
- This action leads to an investigation and trial for trespass, vandalism, and animal release at [TU5](#tu05). It gives the public a credible but false reason to link `L.C.L.B` to the disease's origin.
- Voss is the true origin carrier.
- Voss is not initially an opportunistic criminal: he is a former virologist who recognizes an early viral form resembling his own work in a clue brought back by an `Investigator`.
- Voss creates his `Counter-System` to avoid identification by the organization, then returns to [TU20](#tu20) seeking the virus's original structure.
- At [TU20](#tu20), virology R&D archives contain no trace of an original virus. He understands that Glass Fever comes from the future, most likely from himself.
- At [TU17](#tu17), after joining the study, Voss accesses pulmonary-disease sampling shipments and creates the first strain using his virology expertise and research brought from the future.
- The quarantined fragment at [TU20](#tu20) is the `Counter-System` control ring Voss loses while fleeing laboratory guards. Without it, he cannot return to the `Now`.
- Alice is Voss's daughter. Her public file conceals the relationship under her mother's administrative surname, but degraded medical and genetic data can reveal it.
- The `System` can act only within the Toulouse spatial radius for this protocol. Any external clue must lead back to a Toulouse cause before becoming playable.
- In starter mode, Voss is only the hidden historical cause.
- In complete mode, Voss is an aware `Time Offender` seeking to preserve the sealed-city order.
- Glass Fever must remain explainable at final resolution, but the `Investigators` can expose Voss and preserve cure `Evidence`.
- Completely preventing the epidemic without a replacement cause may make the original `Now` incoherent.

## Lore

In 2142, sealed cities exist because Glass Fever devastated Toulouse twenty-five years earlier before becoming a global crisis. Public archives tell a simple story: `L.C.L.B` conducts breathing rituals and animal releases in Toulouse. Its first visible action is at [TU18](#tu18), when it raids a downtown pet shop and releases every caged animal ([I-TU18](#i-tu18), [F03](#f03)). The virology R&D laboratory participating in the pulmonary study also experiments on animals. After Voss is hired, `L.C.L.B` vandalizes the laboratory at [TU17](#tu17), releases experimental animals, and wrecks equipment. The intrusion targets animals and the laboratory, not pathogens ([I-TU17](#i-tu17), [F03](#f03)). Dana later interviews Ines Valcay, an `L.C.L.B` survivor familiar with symbols, cages, and releases but not pathogens ([TU13](#tu13), [I-TU13](#i-tu13), [F03](#f03)). Accusations against `L.C.L.B` then become the official catastrophe origin ([TU12](#tu12), [I-TU12](#i-tu12), [F07](#f07)). At [TU5](#tu05), a newspaper article revives the laboratory vandalism, investigation, and trial ([I-TU05](#i-tu05), [F07](#f07)). This account is false but useful to the `Now`: it gives the catastrophe a readable origin, protects Voss, and helps sealed cities justify their health authority.

The true origin is Doctor Ilya Voss. Before the sealed cities, he was a biological virology expert. In the `Now`, the crisis forces him to become the `Investigators'` physician. When an `Investigator` returns with a clue tied to an early Glass Fever form, Voss recognizes a signature resembling his earlier virology work. Realizing a personal link to the virus, he creates a `Counter-System` to evade identification by the organization, then returns to [TU20](#tu20) to find the pathogen's original structure.

At [TU20](#tu20), Voss infiltrates the virology R&D archives. The files contain no credible original virus: no parent strain, initial protocol, or earlier sample chain. He concludes that Glass Fever does not yet exist and comes from the future, almost certainly from his own work. Guards hear him in the quarantine room. While fleeing, he loses his `Counter-System` control ring, equivalent to the rings `Investigators` use to communicate with and control the `System`. It is confiscated as the laboratory's quarantined fragment ([I-TU20](#i-tu20), [F01](#f01)). Without it, he cannot return to the `Now`.

Trapped in the past, Voss understands that his reality depends on the catastrophe: without Glass Fever, sealed cities do not form ([TU02](#tu02), [I-TU02](#i-tu02), [F11](#f11)); he never meets his wife in a sealed city; Alice is never born; the council never authorizes the mission ([TU01](#tu01), [I-TU01](#i-tu01), [F12](#f12)); and Glass Fever Protocol never reaches the `Investigators` ([TU00](#tu00), [I-TU00](#i-tu00), [F12](#f12)).

Voss uses his knowledge of the `Main Timeline` to reproduce events instead of preventing them. Toulouse's health network launches a pulmonary study that creates a legal sample route ([TU19](#tu19), [I-TU19](#i-tu19), [F02](#f02)). The virology R&D laboratory participates and provides animal lung-tissue protocols. Voss waits for an entry point, joins the study at [TU17](#tu17) as logistics manager, and gains access to pulmonary-disease sampling shipments. Using his virology expertise and fragments of future research, he creates the first Glass Fever strain in the sampling chain ([I-TU17](#i-tu17), [F04](#f04)). The same `Time Unit` contains `L.C.L.B` vandalism: released experimental animals, open cages, damaged equipment, and activist traces. This creates judicial noise around the right place, but not the disease.

Unstable traces appear before the `Investigators` understand the case. Alice appears in a degraded intake file, evidence that the `System` has already touched the case and that her parentage has been concealed in the `Now` archives ([TU16](#tu16), [I-TU16](#i-tu16), [F12](#f12)). Charlie then notices impossible timestamps proving that `Counter-System` pressure protects the origin chain ([TU14](#tu14), [I-TU14](#i-tu14), [F01](#f01)).

In Toulouse, Bob finds contradictory cargo routes indicating an unstable logistics chain ([TU15](#tu15), [I-TU15](#i-tu15), [F02](#f02)). Voss then secures the medical shipment that becomes the true origin vector ([TU11](#tu11), [I-TU11](#i-tu11), [F04](#f04)). A cargo handler is exposed and develops the first glass cough ([TU10](#tu10), [I-TU10](#i-tu10), [F05](#f05)). Once symptoms cannot be hidden, an emergency order closes the affected Toulouse area, isolates evidence, and turns Toulouse into the official origin scene ([TU09](#tu09), [I-TU09](#i-tu09), [F06](#f06)).

The first clinic identifies crystalline lung tissue and preserves samples that can become cure `Evidence` ([TU08](#tu08), [I-TU08](#i-tu08), [F09](#f09)). Voss knows this `Evidence` threatens him if it reveals the true chain, but is essential if the `Investigators` are to stabilize a coherent `Now` rather than erase their reality. He changes his movement identity to leave the origin site without leaving the `System's` Toulouse radius ([TU07](#tu07), [I-TU07](#i-tu07), [F08](#f08)). He then moves the remaining ampoules through a Toulouse relay clinic, giving the epidemic its final route ([TU04](#tu04), [I-TU04](#i-tu04), [F10](#f10)).

Glass Fever reaches three Toulouse districts and makes the original `Now` likely ([TU06](#tu06), [I-TU06](#i-tu06), [F11](#f11)). Alice, Bob, Charlie, and Dana converge on Toulouse under security pressure ([TU05](#tu05), [I-TU05](#i-tu05), [F12](#f12)). Glass Fever then becomes global ([TU03](#tu03), [I-TU03](#i-tu03), [F11](#f11)). Sealed cities form under emergency rule ([TU02](#tu02), [I-TU02](#i-tu02), [F11](#f11)). Years later, their council authorizes Glass Fever Protocol ([TU01](#tu01), [I-TU01](#i-tu01), [F12](#f12)). In the `Now`, the `Investigators` receive the protocol: open the `Time Flow` within Toulouse, determine whether the epidemic was inevitable, identify the source, and return with enough `Evidence` to expose Voss without destroying the present that sent them ([TU00](#tu00), [I-TU00](#i-tu00), [F12](#f12)).

## Initial Player Briefing

- The `Now` is 2142.
- Glass Fever officially begins in Toulouse in 2117.
- The `System` can reopen causal probabilities only within its Toulouse spatial radius; the scenario remains confined to the city.
- Public archives accuse `L.C.L.B`.
- `L.C.L.B` first raided a downtown pet shop, then vandalized a virology R&D lab tied to the pulmonary study. Early archives mention animals, wreckage, and a trial, not pathogens.
- Several archive timestamps are impossible.
- Ilya Voss appears in clean records but never in public accusations.
- The quarantined fragment resembles a `System` control ring but predates every official version of the device.
- Alice's medical file contains corrupted parentage fields and unresolved genetic markers.
- Identify the true origin, preserve cure `Evidence`, and avoid erasing the sealed-city `Now`.

## Contamination Risk

When an Investigator enters a Time Unit where the Glass Fever is known to be present, the GM asks for a contamination test with the percentile d10. In this scenario, that mainly applies to Time Units 10, 9, 8, 6, 4, and 3. The GM can also apply it to Time Units 7, 5, 2, or the Now if the scene directly exposes the character to an open infectious zone, patient, sample, or contaminated ampoules.

Use the threshold according to exposure:

| Exposure | Threshold |
|---|---:|
| Brief scene with plausible precautions | Easy `20` |
| Clinic scene, contaminated district, or indirect contact | Average `50` |
| Direct contact with patient, crystalline tissue, ampoule, or broken seal | Hard `70` |

The character's Mental Load is subtracted from the raw percentile result, as with other percentage action rolls. On success, the character is not contaminated. On failure, mark **Contaminated - Glass Fever** as a personal Minor Conflict. This conflict can be resolved through quarantine, medical protocol, Evidence of non-exposure, branch correction, or a coherent replacement cause.

As long as an Investigator is contaminated, they must make a health test with the percentile d10 for every significant action. The threshold is normally average `50`, or hard `70` for physical action, flight, combat, a contaminated atmosphere, or sample handling. On failure, the disease impacts Health: the GM rolls `d4` damage, or `d6` if the action was difficult or violent.

If a contaminated Investigator returns to a Time Unit where the disease is not present, especially Time Units 20 through 11, every meaningful encounter with an unprotected person requires a transmission test with the percentile d10. Use easy `20` for a short interaction, average `50` for close conversation or an enclosed space, and hard `70` for physical, medical, or prolonged contact. On failure, the encountered person is contaminated and the GM notes **Anachronistic infection** as a branch conflict. If that infection affects a key NPC or remains unresolved at Merge, it becomes a Major Conflict because the Investigators introduced Glass Fever before its origin chain.

## Possible Endings

Complete convergence:

- Voss is exposed, `L.C.L.B` is cleared of the pathogen despite its vandalism, the epidemic remains explainable, cure `Evidence` survives, and Alice can exist without denying what she learned.

Incomplete convergence:

- `L.C.L.B` is cleared, but Voss is not fully proven, the sample chain remains incomplete, or Alice's parentage remains an unstabilized clue.

Psychological divergence:

- One or more `Investigators` remember a prevented or altered epidemic that cannot exist in the final `Now`. For Alice, this may take the form of incompatible memories of her father.

- The group prevents the epidemic so completely that the sealed-city `System`, the original mission, and Alice's birth no longer have a coherent cause.
