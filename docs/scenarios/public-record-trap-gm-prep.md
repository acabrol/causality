# Public Record Trap - Game Master Prep

This original **Causality** case presents a public-record mystery where ordinary bureaucracy becomes a causal weapon.

## Scenario Premise

Tom Arlen asks the Investigators for help. For a week, public records have placed him in buildings he never chose to enter: a clinic volunteer log, a city hearing transcript, a restricted archive badge, and a charity receipt. Each record is mundane and verifiable. Together, they form a chain of commands.

The apparent source is Nia Rusk, a municipal data intern who processed the records. She is a false suspect. The true source is Registrar Helena Orvek, a **Time Offender** using a **Counter-System** embedded in a public-record indexing machine. Orvek turns ordinary bureaucracy into a causal weapon: if a person is recorded often enough, the System pressures them to become the person the records describe.

## Core Game Master Truth

- Tom is a target, not a fraud.
- Nia Rusk processed the records but did not choose them.
- Registrar Helena Orvek is the **Time Offender**.
- The records are not predictions; they are possible-state records forced into the Main Timeline.
- Orvek wants to identify Investigators through impossible correction attempts.
- The chain stops when the records are reclassified as manipulated evidence instead of accepted identity facts.
